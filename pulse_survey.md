# Manager Pulse Survey (8 questions, ~2 minutes)

**Scale for Q1-Q7:** 1 = Strongly disagree, 5 = Strongly agree. Anonymous; results shown only for teams of 5+.

1. My manager gives me clear priorities.
2. My manager gives me feedback I can act on.
3. My manager recognizes good work.
4. I know what I need to do to reach the next level.
5. I have had a meaningful career conversation in the last 6 months.
6. I feel safe raising problems or disagreeing with my manager.
7. I would stay on this team if I got an offer elsewhere for similar pay.
8. (Open text) One thing my manager could start, stop or continue to help me do my best work.

## Scoring
- **Manager Effectiveness Index** = average of Q1, Q2, Q3, Q6 (1-5).
- **Career-Clarity Index** = average of Q4, Q5 (1-5).
- **Retention Signal** = Q7.

| Score | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 4.0+ | Strong | Ask them to share practices in manager circles |
| 3.2-3.9 | Mixed | Peer coaching, review open-text themes with them |
| Below 3.2 | Needs support | Coaching plan, career conversations within 30 days, re-pulse at 90 days |

## Rules of use
- Use results to **support** managers, not to punish them.
- Share team-level themes back with the team within 2 weeks, or participation drops.
- Never report teams under 5 responses.
- Track change over time, not one-off scores.

## Mapping to the dashboard
The dashboard's `engagement_score` column can be fed by the Manager Effectiveness Index. `months_since_promotion` and `left_within_12m` come from the HRIS.
