How to read this (and what it can't tell you)

  • These are associations, not proof of cause. A weak team can also reflect a hard market, a tough product, or a recent reorg.
  • Teams under 5 people are hidden at manager level to protect privacy and avoid noisy averages.
  • Use it to decide where to look and support first, then confirm with conversations before acting.
